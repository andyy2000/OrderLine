# OrderLine: guide for the restaurant (English / 中文)

## What it does

When a customer calls, the computer answers, takes the order, and sends it to the kitchen.
You don't have to pick up the phone unless the computer needs help.

Every order goes to **three places at the same time**:

1. **The kitchen tablet** (the screen on the counter). A new order pops up with a sound.
2. **The printer**. A ticket prints with the order number, pickup time, name, phone number, and every item in English and Chinese.
3. **A text message to the customer** with their order number and pickup time (if this is turned on).

The customer pays when they come in. Nothing is charged by phone.

## The kitchen tablet

- Each card is one order. The big number (#0012) is the order number, also printed on the ticket.
- The time on the right is when the customer will come to pick up.
- **Red stripe** = new, not printed yet. **Yellow** = printed. **Green** = you marked it ready.
- Tap **Ready** when the food is bagged. Tap **Picked up** when the customer takes it. The card disappears.
- A card that flashes red means the ticket didn't print in 30 seconds: check the printer, and cook from the screen.
- **Yellow banner at the top: "Call handed to you."** The computer couldn't finish the call and sent it to the restaurant phone. Pick up the phone. The banner shows what the customer already ordered so you don't start over.

## Blue box at the top: "Caller asks: …"

Sometimes the computer doesn't know the answer to a customer's question ("can you make it without peanuts?", "can I swap the egg roll?"). It tells the customer "let me check with the kitchen" and the question appears in a blue box on the tablet. **Tap Yes, No, or Not today, or type a short answer, or tap the microphone and say it.** The computer tells the customer right away. You have about 30 seconds; the blue bar shrinks as time runs out. If nobody answers, the computer apologizes and offers to hand the call to you.

## Owner controls (button at the top right)

- **AI answers the phone: ON/OFF.** Turn it OFF and calls ring the restaurant phone like before. Turn it back ON any time.
- **Pickup wait.** Normally 15 minutes. When the kitchen is slammed, change it to 30 or 40. The computer will tell customers the new time.
- **Sold out tonight.** Tap any dish to mark it sold out. The computer will tell customers it's not available. Tap again when it's back.
- **Blocked numbers.** Prank callers. To block one: open **Calls & stats**, tap the call, press **Block this number**. Next time that number calls, the computer says "please call the restaurant directly" and hangs up. Tap **Unblock** here to undo.

## When the computer sends a call to you

It hands the call to the restaurant phone when the customer:
- asks for a person
- wants catering, delivery, or has a complaint
- can't be understood twice in a row
- wants something not on the menu

The computer hangs up by itself on prank calls, abuse, and callers who go silent (it asks "Still there?" first). It never asks for a phone number: it uses the number the customer is calling from.

## If something is wrong

- **The phone keeps ringing in the restaurant and the computer doesn't answer:** either AI is switched OFF (check the top of the tablet) or the system is down. Calls always fall back to the restaurant phone, so no call is lost.
- **A ticket is wrong:** open **Calls & stats** on the tablet, tap the call, and press **Flag for review**. Andy will check what the computer heard.
- **To change a price, hours, or add a dish:** tell Andy; it takes a few minutes.

# OrderLine：餐馆使用说明（中文）

## 它是做什么的

顾客打电话来，电脑会自动接听、记下订单，然后把订单送到厨房。
除非电脑需要帮忙，您不用接电话。

每一张订单会**同时**出现在三个地方：

1. **厨房平板**（柜台上的屏幕）。新订单会弹出来，并有提示音。
2. **打印机**。打印一张小票，上面有订单号、取餐时间、顾客姓名和电话，以及每道菜的英文和中文。
3. **发给顾客的短信**，告诉他们订单号和取餐时间（如果打开了这个功能）。

顾客来店里取餐时付款。电话里不收款。

## 厨房平板

- 每一张卡片是一个订单。大号数字（#0012）是订单号，小票上也印着同样的号码。
- 右边的时间是顾客来取餐的时间。
- **红条** = 新订单，还没打印。**黄条** = 已打印。**绿条** = 您已点了"做好了"。
- 菜装好袋后点 **Ready（做好了）**。顾客拿走后点 **Picked up（已取走）**，卡片就会消失。
- 卡片闪红光，表示小票 30 秒内没有打印出来：检查打印机，先照着屏幕做菜。
- **顶部黄色横幅："Call handed to you"（电话转给您了）。** 电脑没办法完成这通电话，已经转到餐馆电话。请接电话。横幅上会显示顾客已经点了什么，不用重新问。

## 顶部蓝色框："Caller asks: …"（顾客问）

有时候电脑不知道怎么回答顾客的问题（"可以不放花生吗？""可以换成虾卷吗？"）。电脑会告诉顾客"我问一下厨房"，问题就会出现在平板顶部的蓝色框里。**点 Yes（可以）、No（不行）、Not today（今天没有），或者打几个字，或者按麦克风说出来。** 电脑马上告诉顾客。大约有 30 秒时间，蓝色进度条会慢慢变短。没人回答的话，电脑会向顾客道歉，并提出把电话转给您。

## 老板设置（右上角按钮）

- **AI answers the phone（电脑接电话）：开 / 关。** 关掉后，电话像以前一样直接在餐馆响。随时可以再打开。
- **Pickup wait（取餐等待时间）。** 平常 15 分钟。厨房很忙的时候改成 30 或 40，电脑会告诉顾客新的时间。
- **Sold out tonight（今晚卖完）。** 点一下哪道菜就标记为卖完，电脑会告诉顾客没有了。再点一下就恢复。
- **Blocked numbers（拉黑号码）。** 恶作剧电话。拉黑方法：打开 **Calls & stats**，点那一通电话，按 **Block this number**。那个号码再打来，电脑会说"请直接打餐馆电话"然后挂断。在这里按 **Unblock** 可以取消。

## 电脑什么时候会把电话转给您

顾客有以下情况时，电话会转到餐馆电话：
- 要求和真人说话
- 要订宴席（catering）、要外送，或者投诉
- 连续两次听不清
- 要点菜单上没有的东西

恶作剧电话、骂人、或者顾客一直不说话（电脑会先问"Still there?"），电脑会自己挂断。电脑不会问顾客电话号码：它直接用来电显示的号码。

## 出问题的时候

- **电话一直在餐馆响，电脑不接：** 要么 AI 被关掉了（看平板顶部），要么系统坏了。电话永远会转回餐馆电话，不会漏掉。
- **小票不对：** 在平板上打开 **Calls & stats（通话记录）**，点那一通电话，按 **Flag for review（标记检查）**。Andy 会去看电脑听到了什么。
- **要改价钱、营业时间，或加菜：** 告诉 Andy，几分钟就能改好。
